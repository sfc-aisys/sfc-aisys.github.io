AI Systems Laboratory

Kawashima Laboratory, Shonan Fujisawa Campus, Keio University

Dramatic advances in AI technology are substantially changing our lives. Whereas the skills once required for detailed work are becoming unnecessary, a broad perspective on systems as a whole is growing in importance. Our laboratory focuses on systems for the “Post-AI era,” in which the presence of AI is taken for granted. We take on the challenge of exploring the design and operation of diverse systems in areas such as hardware, databases, astronomy, transactions, and art. We invite you to join us in opening up uncharted territory.

Principal Investigator

Hideyuki Kawashima
