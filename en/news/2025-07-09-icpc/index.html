Icpc

Jul 09, 2025

2025

A team with participation from our lab won first place in the domestic preliminary of ICPC 2025. ICPC is an international programming contest between universities, in which teams of three compete on behalf of their university. The team had two members from SFC and one from Yagami. This is the first time Keio University has won the domestic preliminary.
